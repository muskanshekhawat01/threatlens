    SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

    How ThreatLens Evaluates Threats

    01

    Ingestion & Normalization

    De-fanging cleanups (hxxp://, [.]), RFC email header parsing, and recursive embedded URL extraction.

    02

    Dual-Engine Analysis

    Parallel processing through the Probabilistic ML classifier and the Deep Lexical / Typosquatting engine.

    03

    Heuristic Corroboration

    Cross-referencing domain entropy, suspicious TLD reputations, and stylometric shouting indicators.

    04

    Threat Scoring & Guidance

    Synthesis into an explainable 0–100 threat score with itemized findings and concrete safety recommendations.

    Private by design. Honest about the limits.

    ThreatLens uses local, rule-based checks. A score is not a calibrated probability, and a low score cannot guarantee that a link or message is safe. Verify unexpected requests independently through trusted channels.
